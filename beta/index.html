    Offline use on iPhone

    In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Open the Home Screen app while online, then tap Save Hymnal for Offline Use. Keep it open until Ready offline appears.

    Saves all available MusicXML scores, English and Spanish lyrics, and the app. Sheet images are online-only. Test in Airplane Mode before you need it. The app checks for updates each time it opens. A notice at the bottom offers new or changed files only. Offline or failed checks leave the saved hymnal working. If iPhone storage is cleared, download again.
